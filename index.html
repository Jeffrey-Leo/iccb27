A Premier Forum for
Cancer Research

The International Conference on Cancer Biology 2027 (ICCB 2027) is a landmark scientific event organized by the Hyundai Centre for Cancer Genomics and the National Cancer Tissue Biobank at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. This conference brings together pioneering researchers, clinicians, and industry leaders from around the world to advance our collective understanding of cancer biology.

Themed around Molecular Mechanisms, Genomics & Novel Therapeutics, ICCB 2027 will serve as a high-impact platform for presenting breakthrough discoveries, fostering global collaborations, and translating cutting-edge research into clinical applications that can change the course of cancer treatment worldwide.

The conference features world-renowned keynote lectures, thematic symposia, poster sessions, workshops, and dedicated networking opportunities designed to catalyze new research directions and accelerate the development of next-generation cancer therapies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attending ICCB 2027.

Who can attend ICCB 2027? +

ICCB 2027 is open to cancer biologists, oncologists, molecular biologists, clinical researchers, PhD students, postdoctoral fellows, biotech professionals, and anyone with a passion for advancing cancer research. International attendees are warmly welcomed.

What are the registration fees? +

Registration fees will be announced when the portal opens. We will offer tiered pricing for faculty, industry professionals, postdoctoral researchers, and students (domestic and international). Early bird discounts will be available for a limited period.

How do I submit an abstract? +

Abstract submission will be conducted through our online portal. Abstracts should be within 300 words and follow the provided template covering background, methods, results, and conclusions. Accepted abstracts may be considered for oral or poster presentations.

Is accommodation available on campus? +

Yes, limited on-campus accommodation is available for international delegates at the IIT Madras guest house. A list of nearby hotels and partner accommodations in Chennai will be provided in the conference brochure. We recommend booking early.

How do I travel to IIT Madras? +

IIT Madras is well-connected. Chennai International Airport (MAA) is approximately 12 km away. The campus is accessible via Chennai Metro (IIT Station on the Red Line), cab/auto services (Uber/Ola), and bus services. Shuttle arrangements may be provided for registered delegates.

Will I receive a certificate of participation? +

Yes, all registered participants will receive an official Certificate of Participation from IIT Madras. Presenters will receive a Certificate of Presentation. Awards will be presented to best paper and poster winners at the closing ceremony.

Do international attendees require a visa? +

International attendees are responsible for obtaining their own Indian visa. We can provide official invitation letters upon registration to assist with visa applications. India offers e-Visa facilities for citizens of most countries. Please check the official Indian government e-Visa portal for eligibility.

How can my organization become a sponsor? +

We welcome sponsorship at multiple levels — Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Academic, and Media Partner. Each tier offers distinct visibility benefits. Please contact the organizing committee via email for our detailed Sponsorship Prospectus.
